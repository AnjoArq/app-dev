# **Adventure Time**  
## The Magical World of Ooo  
### A Legendary Cartoon (2010–2018)  

----------

> “What time is it? **Adventure Time!**” — *Finn the Human*  

----------

**Adventure Time** is a *fantasy-comedy* series that aired on **Cartoon Network** from 2010 to 2018.  
It follows the adventures of a boy named **Finn** and his stretchy dog **Jake** as they explore the magical **Land of Ooo**.  

----------

### Why I Love It ✨

1. **Creative storytelling**
2. *Unforgettable characters*
3. Emotional and deep themes  

----------

### Main Characters

- **Finn the Human** — brave, loyal, and a hero at heart  
- **Jake the Dog** — magical, chill, and wise  
- **Princess Bubblegum** — genius ruler of the Candy Kingdom  
- **Marceline** — vampire queen with a rock star soul
